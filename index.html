Your browser needs HTML5 Canvas to play Flat Iron Fields.
Controls
  • WASD / Arrows: drive tractor
  • Space: interact with plot under tractor
  • Click plot: use selected tool
  • 1-6: tools   Q/E: crop
  • T: attachment   N: next day
  • B: seeds   U: upgrades
  • M: menu   S/L: save/load   R: reset
